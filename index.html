Presenta Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Presenta ("the app") is a school attendance and communication app operated by ClowTec for partner schools in the Philippines. This policy covers the Presenta Android app used by parents, teachers, and school heads.

Who creates accounts and data

Accounts are not created in the app. Your school provisions your account (a System ID such as PAR-2026-1234) and links it to the relevant student records. Student records (name, section, attendance) are entered by the school under its agreement with ClowTec.

Data the app collects and stores

  • Account data — your name, role (parent / teacher / school head), System ID, and password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider, Supabase).
  • Student data shown to you — your child's (or, for teachers, your students') name, section, attendance records, and scores. This data is collected by the school, not by the app from your device.
  • Device data — a push-notification token (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging) and a device identifier/label so you can see and revoke signed-in devices from the Profile screen.
  • Usage records — sign-in events and administrative actions are kept in an audit log for security.

The app requests no permissions beyond internet access and notifications. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, photos, or files, and it shows no ads.

How data is used and shared

Data is used solely to run the school's attendance, grading, and announcement workflows. It is shared with no third parties, except the processors that run the service:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and server functions.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivery of push notifications.

All traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS). We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

Children's data

The app is used by adults (parents and school staff). Student records it displays are provided by the school, which obtains any consent required under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and its agreements with families.

Retention and deletion

Records are kept while the school's service agreement is active. To correct or delete your data, or to close an account, contact your school or email us at josiahdavid373@gmail.com; we act on verified requests within 30 days.
